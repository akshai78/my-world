About

I design with a focus on clarity, craft, and how things work.

My background in visual communication shapes how I see design, while my curiosity about technology, AI, and design systems pushes me to explore beyond the interface.

I like understanding the problem, challenging assumptions, and turning ideas into experiences that feel simple and intentional.

Journey

From a kid with crayons to a designer who builds.

Scroll — each chapter replaces the last. Hover pins for a glimpse.

Chapter 01 · Childhood

I chased colour before I knew the word design.

Art was never a subject — it was how I made sense of the world. Sketchbooks filled faster than notebooks. Lines, light, and little invented worlds followed me home from school.

Home · Chennai

Chapter 02 · College of Fine Arts

Where craft met intention.

At the Government College of Fine Arts, Chennai, the pencil sharpened into a practice. Form, composition, and visual language stopped being instincts and became tools. This is where my design journey truly began — not as decoration, but as a way of seeing.

College of Fine Arts · Chennai

Chapter 03 · UX Mint

Design as a problem solver.

UX Mint taught me that beauty without clarity is incomplete. Research, flows, friction, and empathy — the academy rewired how I think. Suddenly every screen was a question: what are we solving, and for whom?

UX Mint · Academy

Chapter 04 · The in-between

I wandered looking for the right room.

After the academy I roamed — studios, cities, conversations — hunting for a place where craft and product could live in the same sentence. Some doors closed. Some opened halfway. The map got denser, and so did my taste.

Roaming · Finding home

Chapter 05 · Zoho

Now I design the whole stack of feeling.

Today I am a designer at Zoho — moving across illustration, UI, motion, and even code. One day a visual system; the next a prototype that ships. The craft I started with as a kid still sits under everything.

Zoho · Building

Chapter 06 · Still writing

The journey will continue…

Nothing here is a finish line. New tools, new problems, new rooms to walk into. The map is unfinished on purpose — there is always another pin waiting.

Wherever next leads
